A Practical Guide to Starting a Business

5. Case Study: Fuerte & Hass Avocado Trade

Starting in April, the avocado business performs exceptionally well. This model focuses on sourcing from the field and supplying factories in Thika.

Market Dynamics (As of Jan 2026)

  • Seasonality: The business peaks from April onwards.
  • Variety Pricing: Fuerte starts at approx. 12/= per kg at the farm, while Hass starts at approx. 15/= per kg.
  • Market Destination: Thika is the primary hub with at least 3 major factories.
  • Factory Rates: Prices start at approx. 24/= per kg. While they vary, they rarely drop below 21/= per kg.

Operational Requirements

  • Capital: A starting capital of approximately 50,000/= is recommended.
  • Equipment: You need sturdy sacks and a reliable weighing machine.
  • Permits: Ensure you have the necessary certificates for the collection and transportation of agricultural produce.
  • Labour: Typically requires at least 3 people for harvesting and loading (costing 700/= to 1000/= per day).
